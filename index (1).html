FAQs

Things people usually ask

I've already tried trainers and classes. Why would this be different?

Most people who come to me have been through exactly that. The difference is this isn't a one-off session where you get some advice and then you're on your own. It's one-to-one, in your home and your neighbourhood, with a plan to work from and WhatsApp support between sessions.

What does it cost?

Every dog and every household is different, so I price the programme around what you actually need. I'll walk you through it on the free call, once I understand what you're dealing with. There's no obligation, and you can spread payment via PayPal's Pay in 3.

How long until I see results?

Most clients see a difference after the second session. More complex issues — reactivity in particular — take longer, and depend on consistency between sessions as much as during them.

Are you a dog trainer or a behaviourist?

Both. I train dogs and I run a separate behaviour programme for reactivity and other problem behaviour. The approach is built around the individual case, using balanced, evidence-based methods. No vet referral needed — though if I think pain or a medical issue may be playing a part, I'll say so and suggest a vet check alongside our work.

What breeds do you work with?

Any breed. I've worked with everything from Chihuahuas to German Shepherds. The programme is tailored to your dog, whatever they are.
