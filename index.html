Meet Anna

The person behind Denzel's Dog Training.

Hi, I'm Anna, founder of Denzel's Dog Training. I spent many years in higher education and was a Senior Lecturer in Theatre before retraining as a dog trainer. My passion for helping people learn and grow eventually led me down a different path: supporting dog owners who are struggling with challenging behaviour and want to build a better relationship with their dogs.

I specialise in helping owners navigate one of the most difficult issues in dog ownership: reactivity. I named my business after my own rescue Staffy, Denzel. When I first adopted him, he was reactive and challenging to handle at times. Through patience, clear communication and consistent training, he became the wonderful companion he is now. He remains my pride and joy, and the foundation of everything I do.

Today, I help owners move from frustration and uncertainty to confidence and calm, using practical training that makes sense to both dogs and humans in single and multi-dog households. If a reactive rescue Staffy can do it, your dog can too.

FAQs

Things people usually ask

I've already spent a fortune on dog trainers. Why should this be any different?

Most people who come to me have been through the same thing. The difference is this isn't a one-off session where you get some advice and then you're on your own. It's structured with 1:1 attention, a customised plan to work from between sessions, and WhatsApp support throughout.

What breeds does this work for?

Any breed. I've worked with everything from Chihuahuas to German Shepherds. The programme gets tailored to your dog, whatever they are.

I've tried everything. Nothing has worked.

I hear this a lot. But most people have only tried a handful of common methods that aren't tailored to their specific dog. This is different because it's built around your dog's issues, your environment, and your daily routine. Not a generic approach.

Can't I just figure this out on my own?

You could try more YouTube videos or Facebook advice. But how long have you been trying already? If you wanted results by now, you'd have them. This gives you a proven plan and someone in your corner the whole way through, so you can stop guessing.

Are you a dog trainer or a dog behaviourist?

Both. I work as a dog trainer and offer dog behavioural modification training for reactive dogs and other problem behaviour. The approach is always built around the individual case, using balanced, evidence-based methods. There is no single recipe. What matters is the outcome: a calm, confident dog, and an owner who knows how to keep it that way.

How long does it take to see results?

Though each case is unique, most clients see results after the first or second session, which is very encouraging! Owners leave each lesson with practical tools and a clearer understanding of their dog's behaviour. More complex behavioural issues like reactivity will likely take more time and consistency in approach. Patience and regular training are key to long-term success, and the coaching I provide supports owners in maintaining their own home practice to keep up momentum. By the end of the programme, you'll feel confident in how to read your dog, get ahead of problems, and handle situations that used to feel overwhelming.

What does it cost?

Every case is different, so every programme is priced for the dog, the owner, and the outcome we're agreeing to deliver. I'll walk you through pricing on the application call once we understand what you're dealing with. You can pay via PayPal's Pay in 3 option if payment in full is an issue.
